Framework calibration notes
The infrastructure score weights are calibrated for a capital growth / rentvesting strategy with a 5–10 year hold. TOD/rail proximity (25pts) is the dominant driver — completed Metronet infrastructure is a permanent repricing catalyst. Policy anchors (20pts) reflect state government intent — Strategic Metropolitan Centre designation means state overrides local council on zoning. Supply constraint and employment diversity act as defensive factors preventing downside. If you're targeting a yield-first strategy, consider reweighting the framework — vacancy rate and gross yield become more important than rail proximity.
Infrastructure-led framework
Five-layer decision filter — applied to every acquisition before shortlisting
How the framework works
Each property is scored across five layers totalling 100 points. A score of 70+ is a strong buy signal. 50–69 warrants further investigation. Below 50 is a pass for a capital growth strategy.
1
State policy anchors 20 pts
WA State Infrastructure Programme projects within 5km. WAPC Metropolitan Region Scheme designation — Strategic Metropolitan Centre is tier 1.
2
Transit-oriented development 25 pts
Proximity to completed or committed rail station within 800m. Declared TOD precinct status adds the maximum points. Cross-line connectivity (east-west access) as a secondary signal.
3
DevelopmentWA regeneration 20 pts
Active DevelopmentWA redevelopment area means state capital is being deployed to raise amenity, attract private investment, and improve public realm — directly flowing into surrounding values.
4
Supply constraint 20 pts
Established suburb with limited new supply pipeline. Vacancy rate below 2% signals tight rental demand. Greenfield estates fail this layer — competing with adjacent new stock every year.
5
Employment diversity 15 pts
4+ distinct employment anchors within 5km (health, retail, government, education, logistics). Single-employer suburbs are fragile — industry downturns directly hit property demand.
Score interpretation
70+
Strong buy signal
Multiple infrastructure catalysts stacked. Perception discount likely still present. Proceed to due diligence and buyer's agent engagement.
50–69
Watch & investigate
Some infrastructure signals present. Check which layers are scoring low and assess whether a specific catalyst is incoming. Good for yield-focused strategy.
<50
Pass — for growth strategy
Insufficient infrastructure catalysts for a capital growth mandate. May still suit a cashflow or owner-occupier play. Do not shortlist for rentvesting.
Key disqualifiers (auto-fail conditions)
FAIL Greenfield / masterplanned estate — new supply pipeline disqualifies capital growth thesis
FAIL Vacancy above 5% — rental demand is insufficient to support price growth
FAIL Single employer anchor — fragile demand base, exposed to industry cycle
WATCH Rail station planned but unfunded — treat as speculative, not confirmed catalyst
WATCH No WAPC designation above district level — lower planning uplift potential
Research sources
Primary sources — check quarterly. Bookmark these for your regular research cadence.
WA government & planning
WA Infrastructure Pipeline Updated biannually. All approved state government projects by sector. Filter for transport, health, education. WAPC — Planning Commission Strategic plan 2025–29, MRS designations, TOD precinct declarations, activity centre hierarchy. DevelopmentWA Projects Five active redevelopment areas: Central Perth, Subiaco, Midland, Armadale, Scarborough. Check for new precinct announcements. Metronet Program complete Feb 2026. Monitor for any new phase announcements and station precinct improvement plans. WA State Budget Annual. $10.3B regional infrastructure pipeline over 4 years in 2025–26 budget. New project announcements here first.
Federal
Infrastructure Australia 2026 Priority List — medium (2–4yr) and long (5–10yr) pipelines. Federal funding commitment = low delivery risk. Department of Infrastructure Project approvals and media releases on jointly funded state/federal projects.
Market data
REIWA Market Data WA's authoritative market data source. Median prices, rental yields, days on market, suburb profiles. Free tier sufficient for shortlisting. SQM Research — Vacancy Rates Suburb-level vacancy data, listing volumes, vendor discounting. Critical input before any investment purchase. Free tier available. CoreLogic (Cotality) Consumer Free suburb reports with limited comparable sales. RP Data Professional from $149/month for full data access when buying. PropTrack REA Group's analytics platform. Suburb demand scores, rental demand ratings, price estimates. Integrated into realestate.com.au. ABS Residential Property Price Index Quarterly. Capital city and regional breakdowns. Use for macro trend validation, not individual property decisions.
