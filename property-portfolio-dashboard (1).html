Framework calibration note
This framework is calibrated for a capital growth / rentvesting strategy with a 5–10yr hold. The TOD/rail layer (25pts) is the dominant driver nationally — completed rapid transit is a permanent repricing catalyst in every Australian capital. The planning designation layer reflects each state's planning authority: WAPC in WA, DPIE in NSW, DELWP in VIC, State Development in QLD, and DIT in SA. Adapt the framework weights if your strategy shifts to yield-first — vacancy rate and gross yield become the primary inputs.
National infrastructure-led framework
Five-layer acquisition filter — applied nationally before any property is shortlisted
The five layers
1
State infrastructure programme 20pts
Major project within 5km from the relevant state pipeline (Infrastructure NSW, VIC Big Build, QLD QTRIP, WA SIP, SA DIT). Federal co-funded projects reduce delivery risk. Planning authority designation — Strategic / Metropolitan Centre is tier 1 in every state.
2
Transit-oriented development 25pts
Within 800m of completed or committed rapid transit. New stations in WA (Metronet), VIC (Metro Tunnel, SRL East), NSW (Sydney Metro), QLD (Cross River Rail, Gold Coast LR), SA (future Seaford extension) all qualify. Declared TOD precincts earn maximum points.
3
Urban renewal & regeneration 20pts
Active state renewal agency presence: DevelopmentWA, Homes Victoria, Landcom (NSW), Economic Development Queensland, Renewal SA. Major anchor investments — hospitals, universities, stadiums, city deal zones — signal sustained public capital deployment into the area.
4
Supply constraint 20pts
Established suburb with limited new supply pipeline. Vacancy below 2% nationally. Greenfield estates auto-fail — competing with adjacent new stock indefinitely. Middle-ring established suburbs with rail access are the sweet spot nationally.
5
Employment diversity 15pts
4+ distinct employment anchors within 5km. Health, education, government, retail, logistics, and resources all qualify. Single-employer suburbs are fragile — Brisbane's mining admin suburbs, Darwin's public sector concentration, and Adelaide's automotive legacy areas are cautionary examples.
Score interpretation
70+
Strong buy signal
Multiple infrastructure catalysts stacked. Proceed to due diligence and buyer's agent engagement.
50–69
Watch & investigate
Some signals present. Check which layers score low and whether an incoming catalyst could lift the score.
<50
Pass — for growth strategy
Insufficient infrastructure catalysts. May suit cashflow or owner-occupier play. Do not shortlist for rentvesting.

Research sources
National primary sources — bookmark these and check on the research cadence below
Western Australia
WA State Infrastructure Programme ↗Updated biannually. All approved state projects by sector. Filter transport, health, education. WAPC — Planning Commission ↗Strategic plan 2025–29, MRS designations, TOD precinct declarations, activity centre hierarchy. DevelopmentWA Projects ↗Five active redevelopment areas: Central Perth, Subiaco, Midland, Armadale, Scarborough. Metronet ↗Program complete Feb 2026. Monitor for new phase announcements and TOD precinct gazettal notices. REIWA Market Data ↗Authoritative WA market data. Median prices, yields, days on market, suburb profiles.
Victoria
Victoria's Big Build ↗All active and completed VIC road and rail projects. Metro Tunnel, SRL, North East Link, West Gate Tunnel. VIC Planning — Activity Centres ↗Activity centre designations, precinct structure plans, development overlays. Homes Victoria ↗Public housing renewal estates and urban regeneration program — often a lead indicator of area transformation. Suburban Rail Loop ↗SRL East (Cheltenham to Box Hill) under construction. Stations: Clayton, Monash, Glen Waverley, Burwood, Box Hill. Trains 2035.
South Australia
SA DIT — Active Projects ↗All active SA state infrastructure projects. Filter by region and project type. Renewal SA Projects ↗Urban renewal and residential land release. Southwark Grounds, BioMed City precinct, Edinburgh Parks. Torrens to Darlington (T2D) ↗$15.4B — SA's largest infrastructure project. Non-stop South Road. Construction 2025–2031. Suburbs: Torrensville, Richmond, Clovelly Park, Glandore.
New South Wales
Infrastructure NSW Pipeline ↗$111.5B commitment over 4 years. Updated with Mid-Year Budget Review. Transport, health, education. NSW Major Projects Hub ↗Search and track all major development applications and state significant projects. Sydney Metro ↗Metro West (2030), Metro City & Southwest to Bankstown (2026), Western Sydney Airport Metro (Aerotropolis, 2026). Landcom NSW ↗NSW Government's land and housing development authority. Urban renewal estates and housing pipeline.
Queensland
QLD TMR — Transport Projects ↗QTRIP (Queensland Transport and Roads Investment Program). Bruce Highway, Gold Coast Faster Rail, The Wave (Sunshine Coast). Cross River Rail ↗$7.848B. New Brisbane CBD stations: Roma Street, Albert Street, Boggo Road, Woolloongabba. Opening ~2029. Economic Development Queensland ↗Priority development areas, urban renewal precincts (Northshore Hamilton). Key for TOD investment opportunities. Brisbane 2032 Olympic Legacy ↗$7.1B venue infrastructure. Key precincts: Woolloongabba, Victoria Park, Bowen Hills, Sunshine Coast, Gold Coast.
National & market data
Infrastructure Australia Priority List ↗2026 list — medium (2–4yr) and long (5–10yr) federal pipelines. Federal funding = low delivery risk. SQM Research — Vacancy Rates ↗Suburb-level vacancy, listing volumes, vendor discounting. Critical pre-purchase input nationally. CoreLogic (Cotality) Consumer ↗Suburb reports, comparable sales. RP Data Professional from $149/month for full access when buying. ABS Residential Property Price Index ↗Quarterly. All capital cities. Use for macro trend validation.
